# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Brands, creators, and marketing teams evaluating a creative and growth partner for content, campaign, and workflow execution.

## Product Purpose

PUSHWebb brings strategy, storytelling, production, performance marketing, and AI-assisted workflows into one connected operating system for brand growth.

## Positioning

The site positions PUSHWebb as the team connecting the thinking behind content to the people producing, distributing, measuring, and improving it—not as a vendor of isolated deliverables.

## Operating Context

The agency operates from Dubai and works across YouTube, short-form content, social media, paid campaigns, and marketing automation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn-style aliases, reusable `/components/ui` primitives, and responsive behavior.
- Preserve the strategy-call conversion path and the existing service taxonomy.
- Keep the interactive COBE globe on the About page.
- Use only claims, locations, services, and proof already present in the repository.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is PUSHWebb. Existing wordmark and brand-mark assets live under `public/pushwebb-assets/logos/`. The user has made the Home and About page language and character the reference for the current redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the current site copy, service descriptions, local studio and workflow imagery under `public/pushwebb-assets/generated/`, location imagery for Dubai, and an existing interactive globe implementation. No additional customer or performance claim should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Connect strategy and execution.
- Make growth systems understandable without unnecessary complexity.
- Keep human creative judgment central to AI-assisted work.
- Show the real work through process, imagery, and clear next actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must remain keyboard navigable, responsive from mobile through wide desktop, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
